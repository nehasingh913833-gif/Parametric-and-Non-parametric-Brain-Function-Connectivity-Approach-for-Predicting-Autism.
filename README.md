# Parametric and Non-Parametric Brain Functional Connectivity Approach for Predicting Autism

Early Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) prediction using fMRI brain functional connectivity. This project benchmarks Parametric and Non-Parametric statistical machine learning models within a unified framework to enable faster, data-driven diagnostic support[cite: 1].

## 📌 Overview & Background

**Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)** is a neurodevelopmental condition that affects communication, social interaction, and behavioral patterns. With its growing prevalence, early and accurate diagnosis remains a major challenge in both medical and research communities. Traditional diagnostic approaches rely primarily on behavioral assessments conducted by clinicians, which are often subjective, time-consuming, and prone to delayed identification.

With advancements in neuroimaging techniques such as **functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging (fMRI)**, researchers can analyze brain activity and inter-regional interactions through **functional connectivity**, offering an objective, data-driven pathway for understanding neurological disorders.

## 🧠 Motivation & Relevance

The domain of brain functional connectivity analysis has gained significant traction for uncovering biomarkers associated with neurodevelopmental conditions like autism. Integrating **Statistical Machine Learning** into neuroimaging workflows enables the extraction of meaningful patterns from complex, high-dimensional brain data.

* **Clinical Impact:** Supports earlier and more reliable detection of ASD, enabling timely intervention and improved patient outcomes.
* **Decision Support:** Automated prediction systems based on brain connectivity can assist clinicians, reduce diagnostic turnaround times, and minimize subjective bias.
* **Unified Benchmarking:** Bridges the gap between classical statistical modeling and modern machine learning to determine the most effective methodology for ASD prediction.
